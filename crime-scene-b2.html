🧠 Detective's Grammar Kit

must have been + adjective / noun / place → a strong deduction about a past state, identity or situation.
must have been + verb-ing → a strong deduction about an activity in progress at a particular time.
Compare: “The suspect must have been nervous.” = state. “The suspect must have been waiting outside.” = ongoing activity.
